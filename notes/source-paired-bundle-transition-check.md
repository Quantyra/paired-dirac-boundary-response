This checks the transition-function algebra needed in `compact-orbit-domain-implies-graph.md` under the bundle identification explicitly stipulated in the frozen [coupled-domain source](https://github.com/Quantyra/quantyra-wormhole-semiclassical/blob/f0eab59063422c80ccc7c8e281170c75cc3afc71/notes/coupled-fermion-domain.md) and [paired-phase source](https://github.com/Quantyra/quantyra-wormhole-semiclassical/blob/f0eab59063422c80ccc7c8e281170c75cc3afc71/notes/stationary-paired-dirac-phase.md). It is not an independent reconstruction of a different physical bundle or interacting state.

In the source's common pulled-back frame, J0=tau_1 tensor sigma_1 tensor 1 is a Hermitian involution commuting with every displayed four-dimensional Clifford generator and anticommuting with the boundary involution chi. Therefore it commutes with every Clifford bivector and every spin transition generated by its exponential. In particular, the polar-to-Cartesian spin lift around the regular orbit bolt commutes with J0; the possible minus sign after a full turn is central. Thus the polar-frame antiperiodicity is compatible with one smooth parallel J0 at the Cartesian bolt, assuming the standard smooth spin lift of the recorded disk geometry.

The source specifies that the pulled-back magnetic bundles agree and fixes the relative unitary bundle map. In local angular patches, write their common U(1) transition as g_ab=e^(i lambda_ab) acting identically on the two copies. It is scalar on the copy and spin factors, hence [J0,g_ab]=0. Consequently the product of a spin transition and this matched magnetic transition also commutes with J0, and the local representatives patch by J_alpha=g_alpha_beta J_beta g_alpha_beta^(-1). On each patch the connection has the common block-diagonal magnetic part and a spin connection built from Clifford bivectors; both commute with J0, so the patched endomorphism is parallel. The signs of outward magnetic charges must be handled by the source's pullback: unequal pulled-back transition functions would invalidate this copy exchange.

Under these specified transitions, the formal paired Dirac expression commutes with J0, including in the regular bolt chart. If the physical realization is the smooth maximal projector realization covered by `finite-cut-elliptic-domain-bridge.md`, the Green pairing then yields D*=-J0 D J0 as closed operators. The compact-orbit graph and heat implications follow for the fixed seed. This last operator conclusion depends on that realization and the actual global connections being exactly the ones specified; it is not obtained from the boundary map alone.
