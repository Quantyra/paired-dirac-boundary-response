# Radial cubic jets needed by the a6 contact estimate

This checks which of the hypotheses in `moving-cut-contact-a6-boundary-invariant-bound.md` follow from the recorded radial weighted construction, for the stipulated product-orbit Dirac model. It does not identify the source boundary or bound a heat remainder.

Let w_eta=c y^3+s_eta stand for each of b_eta, u_eta and v_eta. The fixed-field approximants in `smooth-physical-cutoff-joint-path.md` satisfy, uniformly in eta, |partial_y^j s_eta| <= C y^(4-j)(1+|log y|) for j<=5. For j<=3, y(1+|log y|) is bounded on the collar, so

  |partial_y^j w_eta(y)| <= C y^(3-j),  j=0,1,2,3.        (1)

With a uniformly nondegenerate smooth seed metric, uniformly bounded b_eta through order two, and a stationary radial scalar, covariant derivatives through order three are linear combinations of these ordinary derivatives with uniformly bounded connection coefficients and their first derivatives. Consequently (1) gives the required full covariant boundary jets at y=delta, uniformly in eta. This conclusion uses the particular radial approximants; it is not a consequence of an arbitrary function merely being O(y^3).

The a6 invariant-order argument also makes the background hypothesis more specific. Differentiating an E factor spends weight 2 on v, leaving background weight at most 3. Thus every surviving background factor belongs to the following finite list of types: E, curvature or bundle curvature with at most one covariant derivative; the second fundamental form or S with at most two tangential derivatives; chi and its tangential derivatives through order three. Products of lower-order factors are also allowed. This list supplies a sufficient bound, not a claim that every type has nonzero coefficient. The projector itself has norm at most one.

For the conformal product metric g_eta=exp(2b_eta)g_seed, (1) with w=b supplies uniform coordinate bounds through order three. On a fixed smooth seed collar this gives uniform R and nabla R. In the untwisted geometric Dirac square E=-R/4, so E and nabla E are bounded as well. The same is true with an additional twist only if its curvature and first derivative, in the conformal frame, are independently uniformly bounded. The formula S=sigma K Pi_plus from `product-conformal-robin-from-dirac-square.md`, the conformal K formula there, and stationary radial data give bounded K and S and their tangential derivatives through order two, assuming uniformly controlled seed boundary geometry and projector derivatives. A fixed smooth projector in a controlled orthonormal frame with bounded tangential connection coefficients and their required tangential derivatives supplies the chi bounds. These are explicit geometric and bundle hypotheses, not consequences of the scalar weight alone.

Under those hypotheses, bounded boundary length, and the fixed-cut mixed heat coefficient assumptions, the O(delta^3) a6 boundary and interior-flux estimates of the preceding note apply uniformly in eta. In particular, potentially unbounded fourth and fifth normal derivatives of b_eta are not needed for this isolated *potential derivative*: a background factor involving two derivatives of E would have weight 4, leaving no room for the differentiated potential of weight 2 in a boundary invariant of total weight 5. This observation does not control the undifferentiated a6 coefficient or the geometric response, where higher derivatives can occur.

The argument relies on the stipulated product orbit, scalar potential perturbation, conformal geometric square and smooth mixed realization. The actual twist, projector frame and source domain must be checked; no uniform short-time remainder or exchange of cut and heat limits follows. No Lean certification is claimed.
