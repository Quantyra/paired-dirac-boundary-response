# A joint cutoff bound for the localized ordered bulk term

This extracts an explicit time power from `physical-ordered-localized-first-vertex.md` using `physical-h5-from-mixed-h3.md`. It covers the unweighted bulk-bulk ordered contribution with one smoothly localized cubic direction, under the common finite-cut, H5, coefficient and form-trace assumptions of those notes. It is not a bound on the full physical response or its subtracted remainder.

For 0<t<=1 and p in [t/4,t/2], the cited H5 heat estimate and the local order-at-most-two bulk vertex expansion give

 ||V_v^bulk K_p||_2 <= C t^(-5/2) N(v).                 (1)

Indeed (1+p^-1)(1+p^-3/2)<=C t^-5/2 in this interval; the L2 coefficient and chart-summation constants must be uniform in the cut and approximation. For 0<s<=t/2, the ordered bulk factorization with r=t-s has two such factors at r/2 and the bounded short heat operator K_s. The other half follows by exchanging the intervals and vertices. Therefore

 |Tr_form[q_(u_tail)^bulk K_s q_w^bulk K_(t-s)]|
     <= C t^-5 N(u_tail) N(w).                           (2)

For the smooth cubic tail, N(u_tail)<=C K_u delta^(3/2). Including both orderings and integrating the Duhamel weight t-s over 0<s<t gives

 |R_ordered,tail(delta,t)|
       <= C K_u N(w) delta^(3/2) t^-3,  0<t<=1.        (3)

All finite combinatorial coefficients are absorbed into C; no physical-half or angular factor is inserted. Consequently, for the four-dimensional proper-time weight dt/t,

 integral_epsilon^1 |R_ordered,tail(delta,t)| dt/t
    <= C K_u N(w) delta^(3/2) epsilon^-3.                (4)

A sufficient joint path is delta=epsilon^p with p>2. This is compatible with the p>3/2 condition for the unweighted Green boundary pieces in `finite-cut-boundary-joint-proper-time-path.md`, provided the same physical cut and all common hypotheses really apply. If a different proper-time weight is used, its exponent must be recomputed; if a separate angular factor is applied, its effect must not be double-counted.

This controls only the contribution generated by replacing one direction with its smooth tail in the ordered bulk-bulk term. It does not show convergence of the remaining bulk term, control the conformal-log insertion, the mixed second vertex or restorations, prove the source's t^(3/2) curved remainder, or justify path-independent regulator removal. The displayed power is a crude sufficient estimate, not a physical admission margin. No Lean certification is claimed.
