## Recorded reflection

The source explicitly introduces time reflection τ→−τ on the Euclidean
disk, with no sphere reflection, and a full-Dirac-bundle lift represented
in its radial frame by

    U=1⊗σ₁⊗c_S,

where c_S is the parallel sphere chirality on the monopole-twisted spin
bundle. The source states c_S²=1 and its anticommutation with the sphere
Dirac operator, including the zero modes. In the displayed paired frame,
the matrix relations are

    Uγ_τU=−γ_τ,   Uγ_nU=γ_n,   Uγ_AU=γ_A,   [U,χ]=0.

They follow locally because σ₁ reverses σ₂ and σ₃ while c_S anticommutes
with the sphere Clifford action. The two sign changes for γ_A cancel. The
base reflection leaves the sphere coordinates and the purely magnetic
connection unchanged, so this particular construction does not invoke the
degree-minus-one sphere map discussed in the relative-orientation audit.
The source further states the closed-domain covariance and the regular bolt
extension; these are imported claims, not established merely by the matrix
calculation here.

## Bolt and relation to J

Let S(θ) be the polar spin-frame rotation generated by the orbit Clifford
bivector. U anticommutes with that generator, so

    U S(−θ)=S(θ) U.

Consequently the polar expression for the combined time pullback and U has
a constant Cartesian-frame representative, assuming the source's spin
trivialization. This explains why the polar half-angle sign does not create
an extra boundary condition at the disk center. It is the geometric
reflection lift asserted in the source, not an ordinary orientation-
preserving Spin(4) frame transition.

The paired-phase intertwiner is J=τ₁⊗σ₁⊗1. Locally U and J commute,
since both contain σ₁ and the copy and sphere factors act independently.
They nevertheless have different roles: U accompanies a base time
reflection and preserves χ, whereas J exchanges copies, anticommutes with
χ and intertwines the partner boundary domains. The existence of U does
not by itself prove that the original physical copy diffeomorphism is time
reflection or that its prescribed boundary map is J. The latter remains a
separate source-to-operator identification.
